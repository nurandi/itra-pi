How The Math Works

Your ITRA Performance Index is a weighted average of up to your best 5 races from the last 36 months. Here is exactly how the algorithm calculates it:

  • The 36-Month Window: Only races finished within the last 36 months are counted. Anything older expires and drops off completely.
  • Time Weight: Recent races matter more. A race finished today has a weight of 1.00. After 12 months, its weight starts dropping. By month 36, it drops to around 0.33 before expiring. This is multiplied with the Race Score to calculate the Weighted Score.
  • Scenario Weight: The algorithm tests 5 scenarios (using your best 1 to 5 races). Within each scenario, an additional weight is applied to each race based on its rank:
    • Scenario 1: Race #1 (0.97)
    • Scenario 2: Race #1 (0.99) | Race #2 (0.98)
    • Scenario 3: Race #1 (1.00) | Race #2 (0.99) | Race #3 (0.99)
    • Scenario 4: Race #1 (1.01) | Race #2 (1.00) | Race #3 (1.00) | Race #4 (0.99)
    • Scenario 5: Race #1 (1.02) | Race #2 (1.01) | Race #3 (1.00) | Race #4 (1.00) | Race #5 (0.99)
  • The Final Score: For each scenario, it multiplies the Weighted Score by the Scenario Weight to get the Final Score, then calculates the average. The scenario with the highest average becomes your official Performance Index!

Note: This is why running a bad race will never lower your PI. The algorithm simply won't include it in your "best scenario" if it brings the average down!

How the Simulator Works

When you set a target PI, the app injects a hypothetical "Next Race" into your history scheduled for today. It then rapidly runs the entire ITRA algorithm in the background—testing hundreds of possible scores—until it finds the exact minimum race score required to push your overall average up to your target.
